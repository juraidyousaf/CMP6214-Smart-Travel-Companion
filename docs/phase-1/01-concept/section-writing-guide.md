## Introduction and problem statement

**Heading in the template:** Introduction and Problem Statement

**Shape:**

1. Name the app, the theme, and city tourists on foot.
2. State the problem you can support.
3. Say what is still a hypothesis, including smart glasses as a premium idea.
4. One sentence on method: secondary research, user-reported, no interviews.

**Evidence you can use after you re-read it:**

| Claim you might make | Source | Do not stretch it to |
|---|---|---|
| Many people in one survey looked up attractions after they had arrived | S016 | “Tourists cannot find landmarks” |
| Attraction plans are revised on site, including places near a must-see | S015 | Every tourist, or every culture |
| Looking at a phone can weaken landmark memory compared with glasses, on one short path | S004 | All navigation apps |
| Phone use while walking is associated with slower, shorter steps, especially when reading or texting | S017 | A requirement for glasses |

**You must answer:**

- Which single problem is the checklist about?
- Which sentence is cited, and which sentence is still a hypothesis?
- Will the accessibility issue appear here, or only later with the persona?

**Checklist before you paste:**

- [ ] Every factual sentence has a source you opened
- [ ] F1–F3 are not written as validated requirements
- [ ] No invented statistic
