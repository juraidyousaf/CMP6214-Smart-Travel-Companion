# Market research plan

**Status: PLAN ONLY. No market results are recorded.**

The Week 5 template asks you to briefly summarise initial market research results. This file is the plan for getting results you can stand behind. Write the summary in `market-research-notes.md` after the reading exists.

## Questions

Answer only with a source ID. If you cannot find a source, write “not found yet” and keep looking or narrow the claim.

| ID | Question | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | What published descriptions exist of people sightseeing in cities on foot? | Stops the audience line being only a preference |
| Q2 | What problems do those sources report with mobile information or navigation while walking? | Feeds the problem statement |
| Q3 | What do public app reviews, which you have read, complain about or praise in current travel or map products? | Connects market discussion to competitor products |
| Q4 | Is there published work on paying for travel guidance or offline / premium layers? | Relevant to membership. Absence is a finding if you document the search |
| Q5 | What accessibility issues are reported for pedestrian navigation or mobile tourism? | Feeds the third persona. Do not answer from general knowledge |

## Where to look

- BCU Library Search and Google Scholar, using the strings in `research/ALTERNATIVE_RESEARCH_PLAN.md`
- App listing pages and public reviews for products you also observe
- Official statistics pages, if you need a figure. Cite the publisher, not a news repost

## Boundaries

- A market summary without sources is a hypothesis. Label it that way or cut it.
- Do not invent market size, growth rates, or “most tourists”.
- Reviews are not a survey. State how many you read and how you chose them.
- This plan is not lecturer-approved as a replacement for primary research.
