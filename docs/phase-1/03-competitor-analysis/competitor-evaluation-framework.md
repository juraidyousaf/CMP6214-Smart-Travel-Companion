# Competitor evaluation framework

**Status: instrument ready, unused. This is not a competitor analysis.**

Use it with `competitor-observation-log.md`. The checklist summary is written only from completed logs and screenshots.

## Products you might observe

## Session setup

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 20–30 minutes |
| Device | Your phone. Record model and OS |
| Account | Note whether you were logged in. Do not record passwords |
| Before you open the app | Write the task in one sentence |

Use the same tasks where the product allows, so comparison is fair:

1. Find a well-known landmark in a city you do not treat as home, or a city area you do not know well.
2. Find a second sight within walking distance.
3. Create a walking route that includes both.
4. Notice whether an account is required, and how far you can get without paying.
5. Look for an accessibility setting only if you can open the settings. If you do not look, write “not checked”.

## What to record

Write observations and interpretations in different boxes. “The button took two taps” is an observation. “Tourists would be confused” is an interpretation and needs more than one session, or a published source, before it can be stated as a finding.

| Criterion | Observation prompt |
|---|---|
| First screen | What is offered before any search? |
| Discovery | How does a landmark appear? Search, map, list, editorial collection? |
| Place detail | Which facts are shown? What is missing for someone about to walk there? |
| Walking route | Can you build a multi-stop walk? What are the steps? |
| On-the-move use | What do your hands and eyes do during the walking view? |
| Account | Where does registration or login appear? |
| Payment | Is there a premium path? How far can you document without paying? Do not purchase anything for the assignment unless you choose to. Screenshots of public pricing pages are enough. |
| Accessibility | Text size, contrast, voice, screen reader, motion, or “not checked” |
| Breakdowns | Where you hesitated, mistapped, or could not finish |
| Evidence | Screenshot filenames in `assets/screenshots/` |

## Summary prompts for the Word template

Answer after the matrix:

- What do these products already do that overlaps F1 and F2?
- What did you personally fail to do in a session?
- Did any product show a smart-glasses or hands-free walking mode? If you did not see one, say you did not see one in these sessions.
- What will Smart Travel Companion need to do differently? Mark this answer HYPOTHESIS until your wider reading agrees.

## Integrity

- One session is one person’s attempt. Write in the first person for your own actions.
- Public reviews belong to market research as well, with their own source IDs. Do not merge a reviewer’s words into “I observed”.
- Do not publish internal screenshots of paid content you do not have rights to redistribute. For the coursework appendix, follow the lecturer’s expectation for personal-use evidence and keep files in this project folder.
