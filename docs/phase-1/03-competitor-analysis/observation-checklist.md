# Observation checklist — Google Maps, Wanderlog, Tripadvisor

**Status: not started. Complete this yourself.**  
Use one copy of the session block for each product. Put screenshots in `assets/screenshots/` and file names in the session. Do not invent a session in advance.

Documentary facts already checked are in `competitor-comparison.md`. This checklist is for what those pages do not settle.

## Same task for each product

Use a city centre you do not know well. Write the city before you open the app.

1. Find two landmarks you could visit on foot.
2. Put both on a walking sequence.
3. Start, or attempt to start, walking guidance.
4. Note whether an account or payment appears. Do not buy anything.
5. Look for an accessibility control only if you can open settings. If you do not look, write “not checked”.

Spend about 20–30 minutes. Record the phone model, OS, app version, and whether you were logged in. Do not record passwords.
