The brief requires three personas with different needs, focused on your target audience. One must include an impairment or disability and mobile accessibility considerations (Theil, 2026).

This file is a capture sheet. **You write the personas** after evidence exists and after the lecturer has confirmed how the accessibility persona may be supported. Do not turn the prompts into biographies.

Jobs-to-be-done are captured in `../05-jobs-to-be-done/jtbd-evidence-template.md` and summarised in each persona.

## Rules for every persona

- Give the persona a label you choose, and state that the person is a composite built from cited sources, not someone you interviewed, unless you later do interview them.
- Every goal, frustration, and context line needs a source ID or the label HYPOTHESIS.
- Do not invent quotations.
- Do not invent age, occupation, or nationality to make the card look finished. Include a demographic detail only when a source supports that detail or you clearly mark it as a compositional choice with no evidential weight.
- Different needs means the three cards must not be the same tourist with a different name.
- Screenshot the finished cards for the Word appendix. Store exports in `assets/diagrams/`.
