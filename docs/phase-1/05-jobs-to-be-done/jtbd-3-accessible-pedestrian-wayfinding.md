## Approved job

**When** I am wayfinding with a smart device, before I move and while I am moving,  
**I want to** adjust the speed and amount of guidance, choose among audio, visual, and haptic output, preview the route before I move, and receive live route updates while I move,  
**so I can** access wayfinding information in a manner suited to my needs throughout the journey.

The whole statement is an interpretation based on secondary research. It is not a participant statement. The “so I can” line is your design interpretation. S007 does not state that outcome.

| Slot | What it rests on | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| When | S007: wayfinding with a smart device in real indoor and outdoor settings. 33 of 35 studies used a smart device. Parker et al. (2021). Awaiting your verification | A city-tourist itinerary. S007 is not a tourism study. One person who is blind, has low vision, and is deafblind at once |
| I want to — adjustable guidance, output choice, preview, and live information | S007 review themes: control of the speed and amount of guidance; a choice of audio, visual, and haptic output; a preview before travel; live information during travel. Awaiting your verification | A participant quotation. A claim that every person needs or uses all three output modes |
| so I can | **Your design interpretation, not a result established by S007:** access wayfinding information in a manner suited to my needs throughout the journey | A participant quotation. A universal requirement for everyone with visual impairments |

## Kept separate from this outcome

- S007: some participants found apps reduced what they had to remember. Others reported more cognitive load. Both findings stay. They are not this outcome, and they are not one individual.
- S007: real-time route information was associated with higher success in the studies reviewed. That association is a review finding. It is not the “so I can” line.

## Not in this job

- Smart glasses.
- Premium purchase.
- A fictional frustration. The persona card keeps: “Not reported as a single emotion in the reviewed evidence.”
- S008, WCAG 2.2, as someone’s experience.
- A claim that one interface, one output mode, or smart glasses is suitable for this population.
- S006 and S009. They remain on the persona card as review evidence and competitor context. They do not supply this sentence.
