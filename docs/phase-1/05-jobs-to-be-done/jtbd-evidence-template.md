# Jobs-to-be-done evidence template

**Official heading:** Jobs-to-be-done  
**Template instruction:** Describe the jobs-to-be-done.  
**Status: JTBD-1, JTBD-2, and JTBD-3 approved for the working files.** A job needs a situation you can point to in a source or in your observation log. Persona 1 is `jtbd-1-on-site-attraction-planner.md`. Persona 2 is `jtbd-2-smartphone-navigation-while-walking.md`. Persona 3 is `jtbd-3-accessible-pedestrian-wayfinding.md`.

The brief requires at least one job-to-be-done for each user persona (Theil, 2026).

A job is the progress someone is trying to make in a situation. It is not a feature list. “Use smart glasses” is a solution. A job describes the situation, the progress, and the outcome.

## Method reminder

A usable sentence looks like this structure:

> When [situation], I want to [progress], so I can [outcome].

**ILLUSTRATIVE structure only.** The words in brackets are slots. Do not submit a job until the situation is grounded in a source or in an observation you logged.

You will see jobs-to-be-done attributed in textbooks and articles to Christensen and colleagues, and to earlier outcome-driven work. If you explain the method in the checklist, cite a source you have opened and logged. Do not cite from this reminder.
