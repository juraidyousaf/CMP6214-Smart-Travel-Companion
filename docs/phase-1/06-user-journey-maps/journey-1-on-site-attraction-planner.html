Job. When I have arrived with attractions already planned, I want to look at that plan again and consider places near a main attraction, so I can select or adjust which attractions to visit, using the earlier plan as the starting point. The outcome is a research interpretation of Liu et al. (2020) and Huertas and Orden-Mejía (2022), not a participant quotation.

1. Before the Trip

Goal Interpretation, not a quotation Form an initial plan of attractions before travelling.

Touchpoint · S015 Social media and official channels, as reported for that planning. Social media is an optional influence in this 21-interview sample, not a claim about every tourist.
Thought Not reported in S015 or S016.

2. After Arrival

Goal Interpretation, not a quotation Check the earlier plan and search for attractions after arriving.
Action · S015 and S016 Look again at attractions after arriving, and search for attractions while at the destination. Reported behaviour, not an observed use of an app.
Touchpoint · S016 Reported options, not an observed order: Google, then tourism websites, then Google Maps.
Possible evaluation · S015 Timing is a design interpretation Social media could approve or reject a final choice. S015 does not establish when this happened. Possible influence in a small sample, not a step for every tourist.

Difficulty Not reported in S015 or S016.

Evidence limits still attached

  • S015, Liu et al. (2020): 21 interviews. Most respondents were of Asian origin. That limit belongs to the sample, not to a nationality for this persona. Not a walking study. Awaiting personal verification.
  • S016, Huertas and Orden-Mejía (2022): 371 of 402 people (92.2%) reported an attraction search at the destination. Online recall of a last trip, university-contact sample. No app was observed. Social networks and apps were reported less often than Google for attraction searches. Awaiting personal verification.
  • Not inside the research stages: a premium purchase, smart glasses, emotions, walking routes, or a claim that tourists cannot find landmarks or that existing apps fail. The blocks below are hypothetical. They are not S015 or S016 findings.
