2. During the Walk

Goal Interpretation of JTBD-2 and S006, not a quotation Follow the phone guidance during the walk and still notice the surroundings.
Action · S004 Continue that walk while using the researchers’ smartphone guidance. Not a tourist itinerary.
Separate gait association · S017 Phone use while walking was associated with slower gait and shorter steps. Reading and texting changed speed more than talking or calling. Older adults did not show the same step-length and step-time effects. 22 studies, 592 people. This was not measured on the Canterbury path.
Authors’ interpretation · S004 The S004 authors suggest the phone pulled the gaze down. That is their reading of this prototype on one path, not a finding about every navigation app.
Attention point · S006 A review links easier mobile navigation with less attention to the surroundings. This is not an observation that the Canterbury participants failed to notice landmarks during the walk.
Touchpoint · S004 The researchers’ smartphone prototype. Not a commercial app, a glasses purchase, or a premium feature.
