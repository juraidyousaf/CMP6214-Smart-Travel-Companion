## Job this map follows

**When** I am walking an unfamiliar route while using a phone for guidance,  
**I want to** follow that guidance and still notice the surroundings,  
**so I can** take the next turn without that being the only thing I attend to.

That statement is your interpretation, not a participant quotation. The unfamiliar path is reported in S004. The attention wording is your reading of S006. S017 is limited to gait.

## Published findings

- S004, Lakehal et al. (2023): 20 adults aged 21–39 walked one unfamiliar residential path in Canterbury. The phone condition used the researchers’ landmark-based prototype. That age range belongs to the sample, not to Persona 2. The participants are not described as city tourists. Awaiting your verification.
- S017, Zhang et al. (2023): 22 studies and 592 people. The gait association is separate from the Canterbury walk. Awaiting your verification.
- S006, Ruginski et al. (2022): review evidence about attention. It is not a direct observation of those 20 people. Awaiting your verification.
