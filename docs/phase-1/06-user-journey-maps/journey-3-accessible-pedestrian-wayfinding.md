## Job this map follows

**When** I am wayfinding with a smart device, before I move and while I am moving,  
**I want to** adjust the speed and amount of guidance, choose among audio, visual, and haptic output, preview the route before I move, and receive live route updates while I move,  
**so I can** access wayfinding information in a manner suited to my needs throughout the journey.

## Published review themes

S007, Parker et al. (2021). Awaiting your verification.

- A preview before travel.
- Live information during travel.
- A device that stays usable while the person moves.
- Control of speed and amount, and a choice of audio, visual, or haptic output. These are reported options, not a stage-by-stage sequence, and not a claim that every person uses all three modes.
- Real indoor and outdoor settings. 33 of 35 studies used a smart device.
- The population named in the review is people who are blind, have low vision, or are deafblind. Those groups are not combined into one traveller.

## Kept outside both goals

- Real-time route information was associated with higher success in the studies reviewed. That association is a review finding. It is not either goal, and it is not a personal result for this composite.
- Some participants found apps reduced what they had to remember. Others reported more cognitive load. Both findings stay. Neither is assigned to this composite.
