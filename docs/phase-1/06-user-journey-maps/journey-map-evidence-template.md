## Requirements from the brief

Both of these are in the assessment brief (Theil, 2026):

- At least one journey map for each persona. One of these maps must include a persona purchasing a premium membership.
- The Week 5 submission list asks for three user journey maps, one of which should be a premium smart-glasses feature.

Confirm with your lecturer whether purchase and glasses may be the same map. Until then, plan three maps and mark which moments each one carries.

You draw the maps. A screenshot goes into the Word file. Keep exports in `assets/diagrams/`. Mermaid in `ARCHITECTURE.md` is not a substitute.

## Stage headings

Common workshop stages are: situation, action, touchpoint, thought, difficulty, opportunity.

**ILLUSTRATIVE headings.** Change them if your evidence uses a different sequence. Do not fill thoughts with fictional inner speech. A “thought” cell should quote or paraphrase a source, or describe what you noticed in an observation, or be labelled HYPOTHESIS.
