# Phase 3 — Usability testing report

**Status: NOT STARTED.** Weight 50%. Deadline 18 December 2026, 3:00pm UK time. 2,000 words in the main body, with a +10% tolerance. References and appendices sit outside the word count.

The Week 5 interview constraint does not complete this phase. Unless your lecturer changes the brief in writing, the study needs:

- at least five genuine participants
- at least two data-collection techniques
- qualitative and quantitative results
- a redesign based on those results

| Folder | What belongs here, only after the study is real |
|---|---|
| `testing-protocol/` | Plan, tasks, measures, method rationale with references you read |
| `consent/` | Blank form once the lecturer confirms the process, then signed forms for real participants |
| `participant-data/` | Notes and scores under participant codes |
| `results/` | Qualitative and quantitative results as collected |
| `analysis/` | Themes, metrics, and limits |
| `redesign/` | Screen changes tied to findings |
| `final-report/` | The 2,000-word report draft |

Report sections required by the brief:

1. Concept and application introduction, including how the app differs from commercial products.
2. Usability testing protocol.
3. Usability testing results.
4. Analysis and discussion, including other techniques you could have used.
5. Appendices with personas, scenarios, requirements, HTAs, sketches, and hi-fi pages, including changes after design-sprint feedback.

Do not create participant records, consent signatures, scores, or quotations in advance.
